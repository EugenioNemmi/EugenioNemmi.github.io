I am an Assistant Professor (RTD-A) in the Department of Computer Science at Sapienza University of Rome and a member of the [SystemsLab](https://github.com/SystemsLab-Sapienza) group. I work on security and privacy.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Sapienza in 2022, under the supervision of Alessandro Mei, with a focus on deanonymizing users on the Dark Web and detecting BGP hijacks. I was a postdoc at Sapienza until 2024, and in 2025 a visiting researcher at NYU, hosted by Damon McCoy.

## Research interests

- **Security and privacy.** The gap between how technology is supposed to work and how it actually gets used, and abused, by real people.
- **Trust online.** Markets, social platforms and financial systems all depend on people trusting what they see. I am interested in where that trust breaks down.
- **Measurement.** Looking at what really happens on the Internet, at scale, instead of assuming it.
- **Machine learning and data.** As a tool for security problems, and for questions well outside computer science.

We measure fraud on public blockchains and build tools that make them safer to use. We characterized NFT wash trading on Ethereum {% cite la2023game %}, and we designed and prototyped MARTSIA, which combines multi-authority attribute-based encryption with smart contracts on EVM chains (Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche) and Algorand {% cite marangone2023martsia kryston2025martsia %}.

Funding: *Analysis of token-related frauds on Ethereum and BSC* (Avvio alla Ricerca 2022, PI).
